What the Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity course taught me

Mar 2026 coursework from linkedin

I recently completed the Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity course through Cisco Networking Academy, and it gave me a deeper appreciation for how complex — and human — cybersecurity really is.

A few things stood out to me:

Attack methods are more diverse than I expected. Learning about threats like Remote Access Trojans (RATs), phishing, and other forms of social engineering showed how attackers often gain control not through sophisticated code alone, but by exploiting trust and human behavior.

Human error plays a massive role in security breaches. One of the most surprising insights was how many cyber incidents start with something simple: clicking a malicious link, reusing passwords, or falling for a well-crafted phishing message.

Cybersecurity starts with strong fundamentals. I'm beginning to appreciate how important networking knowledge is in security. My next step is diving deeper into networking fundamentals and practicing with Cisco Packet Tracer to better understand how networks operate and how they can be protected.

This course reinforced something important for me: cybersecurity isn't just about "hacking." It's about understanding systems, anticipating threats, and protecting people and organizations in an increasingly digital world.
