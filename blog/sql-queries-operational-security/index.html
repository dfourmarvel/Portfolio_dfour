Querying like a security analyst

May 29, 2026 sql google cybersecurity certificate lab

The scenario: act as a security analyst tasked with querying an organisation's database to support real operational decisions — identifying machines due for OS updates, locating employees in specific departments, and tracing a machine fault to its owner.

Tools used: MariaDB | Techniques: WHERE, LIKE, % wildcard

Some of the tasks I worked through:

  • Retrieved a full inventory of 200 organisation machines
  • Isolated 80 devices running OS 2 — flagged for urgent updates
  • Pulled employee lists for Finance and Sales departments to support a policy rollout
  • Identified the exact employee behind a faulty machine in South-109 using a targeted query, then expanded the search to the entire South building using LIKE with wildcards

What stood out to me most was how much precision matters. A poorly written query in a real SOC environment could mean missing a compromised endpoint entirely.
