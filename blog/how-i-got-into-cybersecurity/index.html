How curiosity about Wi-Fi security became a path

Mar 2026 origin story from linkedin

Grateful to have received a cybersecurity scholarship through ITExperience and Google Career Certificates, and to be starting the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate on Coursera.

What started as simple curiosity — trying to understand how Wi-Fi security works — quickly turned into something much deeper. At one point, I just wanted to see if I could "hack" a network. That curiosity led me to explore tools like Kali Linux, and from there, cybersecurity became more than just interest — it became a path.

Interestingly, I'm currently a medical student, but I've always been drawn to problem-solving beyond a single field. Completing the Cisco Networking Academy Introduction to Cybersecurity course gave me a solid foundation and pushed me to take this further.

Right now, my focus is on:

  • Building a strong foundation in networking
  • Understanding core security concepts
  • Getting hands-on with cybersecurity tools
  • Exploring ethical hacking

Short term: complete the certificate and apply what I learn through practical work.

Long term: move into penetration testing / red teaming.

Cybersecurity sits at the intersection of technology, psychology, and strategy — and that's what makes it so compelling.
