Building a digital tribute for my mom

Jun 2026 web project from linkedin

For a long time, I had a vision of building something truly special for my mom, Josephine Yaba Dzikunu. I wanted to create a digital tribute that went beyond a standard webpage — something immersive, elegant, and deeply personal. However, doing it justice using purely traditional development methods felt out of reach for my current frontend skillset.

That changed when I embraced "Vibe Coding." Using VS Code paired with GitHub Copilot and Codex, I shifted my role from writing every line of syntax manually to acting as a system architect and creative director. By prompting AI agents, managing the logic, and focusing on the user experience, I was able to rapidly build and deploy a polished, production-ready site that I could not have coded from scratch before.

The result is josephineyabat.vercel.app, an interactive, mobile-first single-page experience designed to celebrate my mom through emotional storytelling and cinematic design.

What the AI workflow helped me build:

  • Immersive UI/UX: a warm gold-and-orange aesthetic featuring an animated welcome reveal, smooth scroll-triggered animations, and dynamic typing effects.
  • Deep personalization: a heartfelt digital family letter, a curated memories timeline, background music controls, and an interactive photo gallery with full lightbox support.
  • Production-grade engineering: beyond the visuals, I guided the AI to optimize for performance — implementing lazy-loaded images, smooth transitions, and solid accessibility foundations like keyboard navigation and ARIA support.

Building this project taught me that the barrier to entry for software creation has completely shifted. You do not need to be a veteran frontend engineer to bring complex, beautiful ideas to life anymore; you just need a clear vision, strong logical direction, and the ability to effectively collaborate with AI.

Seeing my mom's reaction to a custom digital experience built just for her was the best deployment feeling yet.
