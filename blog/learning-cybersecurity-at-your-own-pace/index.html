I used to think everyone understood cybersecurity except me

Mar 2026 origin story from linkedin

I used to think everyone understood cybersecurity except me. I saw people talk about XSS, SQL injection, Active Directory and bug bounties like it was normal. I was still trying to learn simple terms.

I felt behind. I doubted myself.

Then I learned something helpful. Progress depends on steady learning. You move forward when you stop rushing yourself.

Starting with free courses helped me. The Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity course gave me the basics. The Google Cybersecurity Certificate is adding structure. Taking things slow helped me understand more.

You grow when you accept your pace. You stay consistent. You learn one topic at a time.

Most people online are only a few steps ahead or more confident in sharing. Your effort matters more than speed.

Keep learning. Keep moving.
