Who is Daniel Dela Dzikunu?

Daniel Dela Dzikunu, also known as D4 or Dfour, is a first-year medical student (MBChB) at Entrance University of Health Sciences in Accra, Ghana. Alongside medicine he trains in cybersecurity through the Google Cybersecurity Certificate, writing up hands-on labs in Linux, SQL, packet capture and incident triage. He also builds web apps with Next.js and Supabase, including med-match, and publishes open-source code on GitHub as dfourmarvel.
