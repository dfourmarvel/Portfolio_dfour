The Panel

Five seats on a solar PV installation course in the Ashanti Region, two with a guaranteed job. Nine people shortlisted. The model interviews them; arithmetic decides.

Overview

A hackathon submission. Most "AI decides who gets the thing" demos put the judgement inside the model. This one does not.

  • The model interviews: it asks follow-ups, probes vague claims and records what each answer actually established.
  • A rubric decides: weighted scores and plain arithmetic, with no model call anywhere in the decision path. Every pick traces to specific evidence and specific numbers.
  • Two goods, two rubrics: training seats are scored on impact, evidence, ability to finish and reach. The two job placements are then re-ranked inside the winning five on who could not get hired without the guarantee.
  • Safe without AI: with no API key it runs on scripted interview trees and produces the same allocation, because the reasoning never lived in the model.
