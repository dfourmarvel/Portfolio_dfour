Mothers-Day

An interactive tribute to my mother, Josephine Yaba Dzikunu — and the project where I stopped writing every line and started directing the build instead.

HTML5CSS3Vanilla JS (ES6+)GitHub CopilotCodexAI-assisted
visit it ↗ github ↗ the write-up →

Overview

A mobile-first single-page site built as a Mother's Day gift for my mum. It opens on a surprise reveal, then unfolds like a love letter — a family letter, a photo gallery, and a timeline of memories, wrapped in warm golds and oranges with animation guiding you through each section.

The goal

In my own words from the write-up: "For a long time, I had a vision of building something truly special for my mom… I wanted to create a digital tribute that went beyond a standard webpage — something immersive, elegant, and deeply personal. However, doing it justice using purely traditional development methods felt out of reach for my current frontend skillset."

So the constraint was real and worth naming: the idea was further along than the hands were.

How it was built

The answer was to change roles. Using VS Code with GitHub Copilot and Codex, I shifted from writing every line of syntax to acting as system architect and creative director — prompting the agents, managing the logic, and owning the user experience. That let me ship a polished, production-ready site I could not have coded from scratch at the time.

  • The look: warm gold (#d8a24b) through burnt orange (#d66b34) on cream and soft beige, with Cormorant Garamond over Manrope.
  • The experience: an animated welcome reveal, scroll-triggered animations, typing effects, a memories timeline, background music controls, and a photo gallery with full lightbox.
  • The engineering underneath: lazy-loaded images, and accessibility taken seriously — semantic markup, ARIA labels, skip links and focus management.

what I learned

In my own words: "Building this project taught me that the barrier to entry for software creation has completely shifted. You do not need to be a veteran frontend engineer to bring complex, beautiful ideas to life anymore; you just need a clear vision, strong logical direction, and the ability to effectively collaborate with AI."

"Seeing my mom's reaction to a custom digital experience built just for her was the best deployment feeling yet."
