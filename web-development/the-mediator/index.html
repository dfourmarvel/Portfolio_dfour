The Mediator

Five parties, one shrinking borehole. The output is a signed agreement in litres per day, and the model running the meeting never computes a single litre.

Overview

A hackathon submission, built alongside The Panel. The usual failure of an articulate AI mediator is an agreement that reads well and does not add up. Here the arithmetic lives outside the model.

  • Protected floors: water for drinking, cooking and sanitation is set from headcount and cannot be traded away, so a school that opened this year is protected on the same basis as households that have drawn from the borehole for decades.
  • Ceilings on justified use: each party's share is worked out against what its own records justify, not against what it asks for. Asking for more earns nothing.
  • Signing is blocked if the volumes exceed what the borehole can sustainably supply, or any floor is breached.
  • The agreement: volumes, abstraction windows, sealed meters, levies and a graduated response to overdraw.
